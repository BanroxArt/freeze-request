# Sources

Primary sources only. Checked on 2026-11-16.

- GENIUS Act, Pub. L. 119-27: sec. 2(16) definition of lawful order, including "specifies the payment stablecoins or accounts subject to blocking with reasonable particularity"; sec. 4(a)(5)(A)(iv) "technical capabilities, policies, and procedures to block, freeze, and reject specific or impermissible transactions that violate Federal or State laws, rules, or regulations"; sec. 4(a)(6)(B) compliance with lawful orders; sec. 20 effective date: https://www.congress.gov/119/plaws/publ27/PLAW-119publ27.htm
- OCC final rule implementing the GENIUS Act, October 23, 2026, FR doc 2026-21706 (120 days after publication falls after January 18, 2027, so the Act takes effect on January 18, 2027): https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/23/2026-21706/implementing-the-guiding-and-establishing-national-innovation-for-us-stablecoins-act-for-the
- Circle Stablecoin Access Denial Policy (linked from the USDC Terms): purpose, policy statement, the three exceptions, reversals, address-level only: https://6778953.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/6778953/Blog%20Posts/Circle%20Stablecoin%20Access%20Denial%20Policy_pdf.pdf
- Circle USDC Terms, last updated October 15, 2026: Blocked Addresses and blocklisting: https://www.circle.com/legal/usdc-terms
- Circle stablecoin-evm, Blacklistable.sol: `isBlacklisted(address)`, `Blacklisted` event: https://github.com/circlefin/stablecoin-evm/blob/master/contracts/v1/Blacklistable.sol
- Paxos US Dollar-Backed Stablecoin Terms and Conditions, sec. 3.5 (freeze on formal legal directive, partner notification, or sole discretion; seizure only on a binding legal directive; contacts): https://www.paxos.com/terms-and-conditions/stablecoin-terms-conditions
- Paxos PYUSD contract README, Asset Protection Role (`isFrozen`, freeze and wipe, "not ... on its own accord"): https://github.com/paxosglobal/pyusd-contract
- Tether Token Terms of Sale and Service, last updated February 26, 2026 (freeze "as required by applicable Law or where Tether, in its sole discretion, determines it is prudent"; blacklisting addresses): https://tether.to/en/legal/
- FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, named as one place to report: https://www.ic3.gov/
